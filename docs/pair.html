PeckBoard pairing link

This is a one-time link that pairs a device with your PeckBoard. Open it on the phone or computer that has the PeckBoard app installed: on iPhone and Android the app opens by itself; elsewhere use the button below.

This page was opened without a pairing payload. Create a link on your box (Settings → Remote Access → Add phone) and open that one.

Get the app

The link works once and expires about an hour after it was created. In the app, compare the box fingerprint it shows with the one next to the QR code on your box before you tap Pair. Nothing in this link is sent to peckboard.com: the pairing details stay in the part after #, which your browser never transmits.
